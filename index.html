Heritage Landmark Basavanagudi

Vidyarthi Bhavan

Serving South Indian vegetarian classics since 1943. Known worldwide for legendary, thick ghee-drenched masala dosas stacked high on tables.

Signature: Masala Dosa, Filter Coffee
Timings: 6:00 AM – 11:30 AM, 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Historic Legend Malleshwaram

Central Tiffin Room (CTR)

Affectionately called Shri Sagar, CTR in Malleshwaram is celebrated for its golden, butter-soaked Benne Dosa that melts effortlessly on your tongue.

Signature: Benne Dose, Mangalore Baji
Timings: 7:30 AM – 12:30 PM, 4:00 PM – 9:30 PM
Techie Favorite Indiranagar

The Rameshwaram Cafe

The pinnacle of modern South Indian quick service. High energy, spotlessly clean, and famous for ghee-dripping podi idlis and crispy dosas.

Signature: Podi Ghee Idli, Filter Coffee
Timings: 6:00 AM – 1:00 AM (Midnight)
Western Brunch Koramangala

The Hole In The Wall Cafe

A beloved Koramangala neighborhood haven specializing in hearty English breakfasts, fluffy pancakes, waffles, and loaded burgers.

Signature: English Breakfast, Pancakes
Timings: 8:00 AM – 8:30 PM (Closed Mondays)
Cozy Cafe Koramangala

154 Breakfast Club

An easygoing, warmly lit retreat in Koramangala offering customizable omelets, artisanal coffee, and delectable French toast.

Signature: French Toast, Omelets
Timings: 8:30 AM – 3:30 PM
Legendary Stand Shankarapuram

Brahmin's Coffee Bar

No sitting chairs, just pure standing camaraderie. Famous for pillowy soft idlis bathed in divine homemade chutney and iconic filter coffee.

Signature: Idli Vada, Khara Bath
Timings: 6:00 AM – 11:30 AM, 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM

A Cultural Emotion

Why Breakfast is an Emotion in Namma Bengaluru

In Bengaluru, breakfast isn't just the first meal of the day—it is a sacred ritual. Long before tech parks light up or traffic fills Outer Ring Road, the air across Basavanagudi, Malleshwaram, and Jayanagar carries the rich aroma of roasted chicory, simmering sambhar, and bubbling ghee on hot iron griddles.

Whether you stand shoulder-to-shoulder with strangers at an iconic 1940s counter sipping filter coffee from a stainless steel tumbler, or unwind over artisanal pancakes in a Koramangala garden cafe, Bengaluru's morning food scene bridges rich heritage with vibrant modern evolution.
